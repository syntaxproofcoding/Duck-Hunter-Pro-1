Duck View setup

Duck View uses Mapbox's satellite map. It needs your own free Mapbox token. This is a one-time setup.

  1. Go to mapbox.com and sign up with your new email. You can start without a card (trial limits) or add one to unlock the full free tier. There's no prepayment.
  2. After signing in, open Tokens on your account page and copy your Default public token. It starts with pk.
  3. Recommended: create a new token instead, and under URL restrictions add your site (for example https://yourname.github.io) so nobody else can use it.

The token is saved only in this app on your phone (and your private cloud sync, if it's on).
